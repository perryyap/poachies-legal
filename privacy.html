Poachies Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Poachies is a virtual pet game for Apple Watch with an iPhone companion app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your pet, your coins and your Health data stay on your own devices. The only information that leaves them goes to Apple (for weather and purchases) and to Google (for optional rewarded ads).

Information that stays on your devices

  • Your pet and game progress. Your pet, its room, outfits, coins and rewards are saved on your Apple Watch and iPhone. The two devices sync with each other directly. There are no Poachies accounts and no Poachies servers, so we never receive this data.
  • Apple Health data. If you allow it, Poachies reads workouts, workout routes, steps, heart rate, sleep, ECG, blood oxygen and environmental sound recordings from Apple Health, and can save meditation sessions as mindful minutes and workouts you record. This data is used only to show your health information in the app and to award in-game coins. It never leaves your devices, is never sent to us, is never shared with advertisers or other third parties, and is never used for advertising. You can change Health access at any time in the Health app or Settings.
  • Heart and noise check-ins. If you turn on gentle check-ins, the watch reads Apple's heart and noise events to show a friendly message. This stays on the watch. Poachies is not a medical device.
  • Reminders. Meal, sleep, step-goal and growing-up reminders are scheduled on your own devices.

Information shared with others

  • Weather (Apple). If you choose a city or allow your approximate location, that location (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to Apple WeatherKit to fetch the local weather. Location is used only for weather, is requested only while the app is open, and is never used for ads.
  • Workout routes. If you allow Location on your watch, outdoor workouts can save a route map to Apple Health. It is stored in Health, not sent to us.
  • Nearby battles. When you battle a friend nearby, the two iPhones connect directly over the local network and share only your pet's name, species, generation, care stats and outfit. No Health data or personal information is shared.
  • Purchases (Apple). Coin packs are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.
  • Sharing with friends. If you choose to share Poachies, your device's share sheet sends the App Store link through the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). Poachies does not read your messages or contacts.
  • Rewarded ads (Google AdMob). The iPhone app can show optional videos you choose to watch for in-game coins. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, information about your device and how you interact with ads, diagnostics, and approximate location from your IP address, to deliver, measure and personalize ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads load, and you can change it later. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Watching ads is always optional.

Children

Poachies is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Because your game data lives only on your devices, deleting the Poachies apps removes it. Health data stays in Apple Health under your control. For data Google collects for ads, see Google's privacy policy above.
